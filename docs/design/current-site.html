(01) The Studio

TUMACH started as a bad pun and turned into a real floor. Tuma is the family name; "too much dance" is the promise. We couldn't pick one, so we kept both.

The space is intentionally small — one room, a wall of mirror, a fresh black marley floor, and afternoon light from the street. No reception desk, no membership funnel. Just somewhere to move.

We teach contemporary and improvisation, and we leave the floor open for people who just need a place to work things out in their body.

"Is it too much dance?"
Honestly? never enough. — founding principle, written on the wall
